PetMotion Support

PetMotion helps pet owners log pet moments, review mood signals, and unlock optional Pro or Ultra features with App Store subscriptions.

Contact

Email support: roik378@gmail.com

Common Questions

  • Camera and microphone permissions: PetMotion asks for permission only when a feature needs camera or audio input. You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Cloud analysis: Optional AI analysis can be controlled in PetMotion settings, including whether video frames are uploaded.
  • Subscriptions: Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Manage or cancel them from your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Restore purchases: Use the restore button on the subscription screen to restore App Store purchases for the signed-in Apple ID.
